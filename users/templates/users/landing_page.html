What InfraTrack Offers

One System, Every Stage of the Process

From the citizen's first report to the District Engineer's final sign-off, every step is tracked, validated, and visible.

Issue Reporting & Validation

Citizens report infrastructure issues with photos and location; the Frontline Receiving Unit screens and validates each report.

Work Order Management

District Engineers review validated reports and generate work orders for approved repair actions.

Repair Progress Monitoring

Field Engineers log repair updates in real time, so progress is visible from first response to closure.

Geo-Tagged Mapping

Every report is pinned on an interactive map, powered by Google Maps, so issues are located at a glance.

Role-Based Access

Citizen Reporters, FRU, Field Engineers, District Engineers, and Admins each get a dashboard built for their role.

Full Report History

Closed and rejected reports stay archived and searchable, keeping a full record of every resolved issue.

The Process

How InfraTrack Works

1

Citizen Reports an Issue

A resident submits a report with a photo, description, and location pin of a road, bridge, or drainage problem.

2

FRU Screens & Validates

The Frontline Receiving Unit reviews the report for completeness and forwards validated reports to the District Engineer.

3

Work Order is Issued

The District Engineer approves the report and generates a work order, assigning it to a Field Engineer.

4

Repair is Tracked to Completion

The Field Engineer logs repair progress until the issue is resolved and the report is officially closed.
